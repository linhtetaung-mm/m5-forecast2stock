# Raw M5 inputs

Place the original `calendar.csv`, `sell_prices.csv` and `sales_train_validation.csv` here. Obtain them through the [M5 competition data page](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/m5-forecasting-accuracy/data) after completing the site's access steps. Keep original bytes. The default SHA-256 expectations are recorded in the configuration. The raw data are excluded from Git. The calendar may extend beyond observed sales.
